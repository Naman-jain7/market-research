## Adoption Barriers, Trust Concerns & Switching Costs
- **API policy volatility** – LinkedIn, X, and WhatsApp can change rate limits or fees, creating uncertainty for budgeting (market overview)【{"title":"Content Summarization For Video AI Market Research Report 2033","url":"https://dataintelo.com/report/content-summarization-for-video-ai-market"}】.
- **Data‑privacy liability** – Processing spoken names creates personal data; creators fear GDPR breaches. Lack of built‑in audit logs in competitors raises trust gaps.
- **Switching friction** – Existing workflows often embed tools like Lately or internal scripts; moving to a new platform requires re‑training, migration of API keys, and re‑authorizing WhatsApp Business opt‑ins.
- **Pricing uncertainty** – Many creators are on free tiers (Castifai, Eightify) and are hesitant to commit to $30‑$70/mo without clear ROI evidence.

## Evidence Gaps & Validation Assumptions
- **Actual paid demand for WhatsApp‑native posting** – No public data; assumption based on creator surveys stating they share video highlights in WhatsApp groups (anecdotal). Needs validation via interviews or a landing‑page test.
- **Price elasticity for solo creators** – Current pricing signals are indirect (CapCut, Castifai). A willingness‑to‑pay experiment (A/B pricing $12 vs $30) is required.
- **API cost impact on profitability** – Estimates rely on average API rates; real‑world volume per creator varies. Need usage‑based cost modeling.
- **Compliance adoption** – Assumed that a built‑in consent manager will be a differentiator, but regulatory acceptance and user friction are untested.
- **Market size for English‑language North American & European creators** – TAM estimate assumes 20 % of 15 M active channels are interested (medium confidence). Should be validated with a targeted survey.
